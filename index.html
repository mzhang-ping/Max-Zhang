About Me

I am a dedicated 10th-grade student at The Browning School on Manhattan's Upper East Side with a deep passion for engineering and mathematics. From a young age I have been drawn to the way mathematics explains the physical world — the geometry in structures, the calculus in motion, the physics in every well-engineered design.

Driven by a commitment to technical excellence, I actively pursue advanced mathematics coursework on my own time, extending well into calculus and beyond, to strengthen my analytical problem-solving capabilities and build a foundation for a career in engineering.

I have four years of hands-on experience with Computer-Aided Design software, and I hold the OnShape CAD certification — an industry-recognized credential that validates my ability to design complex 3D models, assemblies, and technical drawings. I have used this skill to take independent engineering projects from concept to finished digital prototype.

Outside of academics I am an active member of the Rocketry Club, where engineering theory meets real-world application. Working alongside teammates to design, build, and launch rockets has taught me precision, iteration, and the value of careful collaboration.

My goal is to pursue a career at the intersection of engineering and innovation — whether in aerospace, robotics, or applied mathematics — and I am always looking for opportunities to grow and contribute.
